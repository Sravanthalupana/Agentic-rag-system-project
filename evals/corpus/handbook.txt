Employee Expense and Benefits Handbook

Reimbursements
The reimbursement deadline is 30 days after the purchase date.
Submit reimbursement receipts through the expense portal.
Reimbursement requests over 500 USD require manager approval.
Approved reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

Learning and development
The annual learning budget is 1200 USD per employee.
Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year.

Travel
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.
Hotel bookings must be made through the corporate travel desk.
