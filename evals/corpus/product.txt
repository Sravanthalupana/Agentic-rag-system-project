Product Policies for the Aurora Smart Thermostat

Warranty
The warranty lasts 2 years from the delivery date.
The warranty does not cover damage caused by improper installation.

Refunds and returns
Customers may request a refund within 14 days of delivery.
Returned devices must include the original packaging.

Support
The support window is 9 AM to 5 PM UTC on weekdays.
Premium customers can reach support by phone; other customers use email.
