Platform Operations Guide

Hosting
Production data is hosted in the eu-west-1 region.
The disaster recovery site is in the eu-central-1 region.

Backups
Database backups run every 6 hours.
Backups are retained for 30 days and tested monthly.

Incidents
Incident escalations go to the on-call engineer.
A severity 1 incident requires a public status page update within 15 minutes.
